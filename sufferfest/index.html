Are you ready to embrace the cold and put your amateur radio skills to the test?

The Hoosier Sufferfest is a Winter Field Day QRP operation designed to challenge operators and equipment under realistic winter conditions.

We're calling it a Sufferfest, and we intend to keep the squelch loose! But behind the fun, we're treating this as a serious emergency communications training exercise.

Operating Details

  • Advance registration required.
  • One vehicle per campsite – Site 398 is reserved.
  • QRP operation – Low-power operating only.
  • Field shelters – Tents, bivy shelters, tarps, and bushcraft shelters are welcome. RVs and campers are prohibited.
  • No generators.
  • Camp overnight – Everyone is expected to camp on site, spend the night, and aim to stay for the full 24-hour event.
  • Event callsign TBD – All participants should plan to operate under the designated Hoosier Sufferfest callsign once announced.

The Challenge

The goal is to practice portable amateur radio operations in winter conditions, where cold weather, limited power, shelter, equipment setup, and operator endurance all become part of the exercise. Camping overnight in field shelters and operating without generators are part of the spirit of Sufferfest.

This is an opportunity to test not only our radios and antennas but also our ability to establish and maintain communications when conditions are less than ideal.

Participation

This is a closed operating event, limited to registered participants. We are not accepting walk-in operators, but we welcome all the RF contacts we can make!
